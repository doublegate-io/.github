# Code of conduct

One person maintains this project. That makes an elaborate enforcement ladder dishonest, so
here is the short version, which is enforceable.

## The standard

**Argue with the claim, not the person.** This project publishes an argued case against
itself and asks people to attack its evidence. That only works if the arguing is about
substance — a number, a source, a design consequence — and never about who said it.

Specifically welcome: blunt disagreement, "this is wrong and here is why", pointing out
that a citation doesn't support the sentence it's attached to, and saying the project
shouldn't be built.

Specifically not: personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexualised
language, or persistently reopening a settled decision to wear someone down.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, commit messages and email in this organization.

## Enforcement

Report to [eugene.korniichuk@gmail.com](mailto:eugene.korniichuk@gmail.com?subject=doublegate%20—%20conduct).
Reports are read by the person who maintains the project and nobody else.

Responses are, in order: a request to stop, deletion of the content, and a block. There is
no appeals committee — there is one maintainer, and pretending otherwise would be theatre.

## The asymmetry, stated plainly

Being right does not exempt you from this, and being wrong does not forfeit your standing
to argue. A correct technical point delivered as an insult still gets moderated, and the
technical point still gets fixed — those are two separate things and both happen.
